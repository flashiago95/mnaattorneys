MNA provides senior-led outsourced company secretarial, corporate governance, statutory compliance and board advisory support to private companies, public companies, state-owned entities, non-profit organisations and institutional clients that need practical, reliable and cost-effective governance capability.

The national fraud landscape
### Why it matters
Forensic investigation has grown significantly in South Africa. Investigations are no longer conducted only by the South African Police Service, but extensively by private and corporate bodies and government institutions, and always within the bounds of the Constitution.
Corporate governance and ethics carry growing weight following the Zondo Commission. In the 2021 National Risk Assessment, prepared for South Africa’s Mutual Evaluation, the South African Fraud Prevention Service cited fraud as the second most reported high-risk crime in the country.
Fraud, corruption, theft, harassment and discrimination can cause significant financial, media and reputational damage, and globalisation keeps increasing the reach and intensity of economic crime.
